          Definitions

          One definition for every column and canonical field shown in the dashboard. Hover a row to see its definition as a tooltip.

          Column / fieldDefinition
          ISINThe unique identifier used to join the product across dossiers, evidence, and generated views. It is the most reliable way to trace a row back to its source record.
          SecurityThe displayed product name, supplemented by its structure where a name is unavailable. It is a navigation label, not a complete description of the payoff or risk.
          CohortThe product grouping used to select a comparable cost observation. Cohort membership is an analytical classification and does not establish that the products have identical terms or economics.
          Issue dateThe reported date on which the security was issued. It anchors vintage and regulatory-regime comparisons, but does not establish the Trust acquisition date.
          Lifecycle endThe earliest documented end event used by the model, normally a contractual call or scheduled maturity. It should not be read as proof that the security was actually held until that date.
          End basisThe source interpretation used for lifecycle end, such as documented call date or scheduled maturity. This makes clear whether the displayed date is contractual, observed, or only the reported maturity field.
          Position basisThe control classification explaining whether the reported amount is a usable Trust invested notional. It prevents issuer issue size, minimum denomination, shared line-item amounts, and missing values from being mistaken for Trust exposure.
          Reported position (USD)The usable Trust-reported amount converted or recorded in US dollars for scenario calculations. It is an exposure basis, not evidence of purchase price, current balance, fee payment, or lifetime holding.
          Evidence-only min (USD)The lower end of the cost range produced only from comparators that meet the adoption rules for the relevant cohort. It excludes unsupported components and must not be interpreted as proof that the amount was charged or paid.
          Evidence-only max (USD)The upper end of the cost range produced only from comparators that meet the adoption rules for the relevant cohort. It is a bounded evidence scenario, not a complete lifetime-cost estimate where transaction, service, or exit evidence is missing.
          Proxy Cost_ Embedded product proxy (USD)The one-off or product-cost proxy used as the controlling embedded component. A displayed range preserves the source-supported range rather than inventing a midpoint.
          Proxy Cost_ Recurring service proxy (USD, holding period)The annual investment-service proxy accumulated over the valid holding period so that it can be added to the one-off embedded and assumed-exit components in the displayed total.
          Proxy Cost_ Exit / transaction proxy (USD)The contingent exit and transaction component. Zero means no exit scenario is currently modelled.
          Proxy total (USD)The reconciled lifetime proxy total calculated as embedded product proxy plus recurring service proxy over the holding period plus the assumed exit and transaction proxy. Its displayed components must sum to this amount.
          Proxy calculation statusStates whether the proxy is embedded-only, includes other modelled groups, unbenchmarked, or not calculated. It prevents an unmodelled group from being read as evidence of zero cost.
          Bank HeldThe bank or intermediary identified as holding, administering, or appearing against the position. This is an evidence attribute and does not by itself prove beneficial ownership or the amount held.
          CountryThe country linked to the issuer, product, or supporting evidence. It supports jurisdictional comparability but does not identify the distribution channel or governing law unless those are separately documented.
          CurrencyThe currency in which the denomination, payoff, or payment is expressed. Currency affects comparability and conversion, so a displayed USD amount may still represent a foreign-currency exposure.
          Display OrderThe source workbook or presentation sequence retained for stable review ordering. It carries no economic, risk, or priority meaning.
          Field StatusesField-level labels describing evidence quality, provenance, or review treatment. They allow users to distinguish sourced values from inferred, missing, conflicting, or excluded values.
          GuarantorThe entity identified as guaranteeing some or all obligations under the product documentation. The presence of a guarantor does not remove issuer, market, liquidity, or documentation risk.
          Holding Period YearsThe elapsed period between the documented purchase basis and the modelled end event, expressed in years. It compounds or accumulates annualised comparators for relevant products, but does not turn a one-off issuance comparator into a recurring cost.
          Issue SizeThe issuer-reported total issuance or outstanding amount. It describes the security programme or issuance, not the Trust's investment, and must not replace a usable position amount.
          IssuerThe entity named as issuer in the product evidence. Issuer identity helps assess credit and jurisdictional context, but does not identify every distributor, guarantor, or compensation recipient.
          MaturityThe documented maturity, call, or other contractual lifecycle terms. A call feature may end the modelled lifecycle earlier than scheduled maturity, but neither date proves the realised holding outcome.
          Position Size EvidenceThe document or control rationale supporting the position classification. It explains why an amount is usable, shared, issuer-level, minimum-denomination, missing, or otherwise excluded from Trust exposure totals.
          Position Size SourceThe source used to classify the reported position amount. Source identity supports traceability, but the amount remains subject to the source's scope, date, and evidential limitations.
          Position Size StatusThe controlled status assigned to the reported position basis. Only a usable invested-notional status enters exposure-based cost calculations; other statuses remain visible but are not silently treated as zero.
          Purchase DateCanonical YAML field: purchase_date.
          Source ExhibitThe specific exhibit or evidence item supporting the canonical value. A populated locator improves auditability, but does not guarantee that the source resolves every ambiguity in the record.
          Source SectionThe workbook, document, or section locator supporting the record. It helps reviewers reproduce the extraction and assess whether the source is product-level, account-level, or contextual.
          StructureThe instrument or payoff family assigned to the product. It provides the starting point for cohort analysis, but similar labels can conceal materially different barriers, underlyings, calls, or loss mechanics.
          Tenor YearsThe contractual duration from issue to scheduled maturity, expressed in years. Tenor is not the same as the Trust holding period because the product may have been acquired later, called early, or sold before maturity.
          Term Sheet AvailableWhether a term sheet or equivalent primary product document has been located. Availability supports further verification but does not mean the document has been fully reviewed or that all fields are confirmed.
          TrustThe Trust, account, or portfolio context associated with the record. It identifies the ownership or review context being analysed, but does not by itself prove a position amount or transaction history.
          UnderlyingThe asset, index, interest rate, spread, or other reference variable driving a product feature. The underlying identifies the source of market exposure, not the investor's economic outcome or the product's total cost.
