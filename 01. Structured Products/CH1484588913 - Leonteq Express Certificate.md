## Original Terms Evidence

The local Leonteq term sheet provides documentary evidence for the contractual structure and supplements the workbook reference layer. It identifies the product as an Express Certificate on the S&P 500 and Swiss Market Index, issued in USD on a quanto basis, with a 1.625% conditional coupon, 60% coupon-trigger and barrier levels, and maturity-only barrier observation (PDF page 1). It records the initial fixing date as 24-Feb-2026, issue date as 03-Mar-2026, final fixing date as 24-Feb-2028, and redemption date as 03-Mar-2028 (PDF page 1). The redemption section states that an autocall can cause early redemption at denomination, while a barrier event links redemption to the worst-performing underlying (PDF page 2).

The term sheet names Leonteq Securities AG, Guernsey Branch as issuer and PostFinance Ltd as guarantor (PDF page 2). It also states that the product is not listed and that investors may lose some or all invested capital and are exposed to issuer credit risk (PDF pages 1-3). These terms corroborate and materially expand the workbook description; they do not replace the workbook values.
