## Original Terms Evidence

The local Aquarius prospectus corroborates and expands the workbook description. It identifies Aquarius + Investments PLC, an Irish public limited company, as issuer of US$750,000,000 perpetual limited-recourse secured notes, secured over US$750,000,000 of perpetual subordinated capital instruments issued by Swiss Reinsurance Company Ltd, with settlement for registered shares of Swiss Re Ltd (PDF page 1). The prospectus expressly limits noteholder claims to the mortgaged property and states that the notes are not guaranteed by, or otherwise the responsibility of, Swiss Re or its affiliates (PDF pages 1 and 9). The embedded terms state an initial 8.25% annual interest rate, annual interest payments, a first optional redemption date of 1-Sep-2018, and a specified denomination of US$1,000 (PDF pages 50 and 69).

The Aquarius publication dated 3-Jul-2018 documents the subsequent lifecycle: Swiss Re gave notice that the underlying instruments would be redeemed in full on 1-Sep-2018, and Aquarius notified noteholders that each note would be redeemed in full on 11-Sep-2018 at its pro rata share of the underlying proceeds (PDF pages 1-2). The publication identifies the exact ISIN `XS0765564827` and confirms the Swiss Re underlying instruments (PDF page 1). This documentary date is retained as original-term evidence; it does not overwrite the blank workbook maturity field or silently convert the OCR candidate `09/11/2018`, whose date convention remains unconfirmed.
